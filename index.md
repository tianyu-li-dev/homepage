## About Me

Greetings! I am a Ph.D. student in Supply Chain & Operations Management at Purdue University advised by Professors [William B. Haskell](https://www.wbhaskell.com/) and [Chen-An Lin](https://web.ics.purdue.edu/~lin1800/index.html). I do operations management and [mathematical engineering](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matematik_m%C3%BChendisli%C4%9Fi) for smarter decisions. My current research focuses on developing methods for sequential decision-making and applying them to real-world business settings.

## Research Interests

- **Methodology:** dynamic programming and optimal control, stochastic modeling
- **Application:** inventory theory
- I am exploring applications of AI to decision-making. I am also broadly interested in mathematical questions in analysis, geometry, and probability arising from decision-making problems.

## Working Papers

### Analysis of Certainty-Equivalent Control for a Joint Pricing and Inventory Management Problem with Lost-Sales
with C.A. Lin and W. B. Haskell.

We study certainty-equivalent control without and with reoptimization in a finite-horizon stochastic inventory system with endogenous pricing and replenishment decisions.

### Causal Effect of Prolonged Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy on Mortality of Patients in the ICU: A Retrospective Cohort Study
with R. Yang, T. Shi, J. Ma, H. Zhao and G. Kong. Under Revision in *PLOS Digital Health*.

We use double machine learning to evaluate the clinical consequences of prolonged versus timely CRRT discontinuation.
