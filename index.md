## About Me

Greetings! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Supply Chain & Operations Management at Purdue University, advised by Professors [William B. Haskell](https://www.wbhaskell.com/) and [Chen-An Lin](https://web.ics.purdue.edu/~lin1800/index.html). I do operations management and [mathematical engineering](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matematik_m%C3%BChendisli%C4%9Fi) for smarter decisions. My current research focuses on developing methods for data-driven and sequential decision-making and applying them to real-world business settings.

## Research Interests

- **Methodologies:** dynamic programming and optimal control, data-driven and online optimization, stochastic modeling
- **Applications:** pricing and inventory management, healthcare, AI in business decision-making, digital twins, last-mile delivery and vehicle routing
- I am also broadly interested in mathematical problems in analysis, geometry, and probability arising from decision-making.

## Working Papers

### Analysis of Certainty-Equivalent Control for a Joint Pricing and Inventory Management Problem with Lost Sales
with C.A. Lin and W. B. Haskell.

How well does certainty-equivalent (CE) control perform for a finite-horizon stochastic inventory system with endogenous pricing and replenishment decisions? Can CE control be improved in terms of computational efficiency and policy performance? How do these improvements compare with existing variants of CE control in the literature?

### Causal Effect of Prolonged Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy on Mortality of Patients in the ICU: A Retrospective Cohort Study
with R. Yang, T. Shi, J. Ma, H. Zhao and G. Kong. Provisionally accepted by *PLOS Digital Health*. [\[GitHub\]](https://github.com/tianyu-li-dev/prolonged-crrt)

We use double machine learning to evaluate the clinical consequences of prolonged versus timely CRRT discontinuation.
