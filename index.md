## About Me

Greetings! I am a Ph.D. student in Supply Chain & Operations Management at Purdue University advised by Professors [William B. Haskell](https://www.wbhaskell.com/) and [Chen-An Lin](https://web.ics.purdue.edu/~lin1800/index.html). I do operations management and [mathematical engineering](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matematik_m%C3%BChendisli%C4%9Fi) for smarter decisions. My research focuses on developing methods for sequential decision-making and applying them to real-world business settings.

## Research Interests

- **Methodology:** dynamic programming and optimal control, stochastic modeling
- **Application:** inventory theory
- I am exploring applications of AI to decision-making. I am also broadly interested in mathematical questions in analysis, geometry, and probability arising from decision problems.
