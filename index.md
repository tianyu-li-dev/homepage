## About Me

Greetings! I am a Ph.D. student in Supply Chain & Operations Management at Purdue University under the supervision of Professors [William B. Haskell](https://www.wbhaskell.com/) and [Chen-An Lin](https://web.ics.purdue.edu/~lin1800/index.html). I do [mathematical engineering](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matematik_m%C3%BChendisli%C4%9Fi) and operations management. My research focus is on developing methods and applications of sequential decision-making problems.

## Research Interests

- **Methodology:** dynamic programming and optimal control
- **Applications:** stochastic modeling, inventory theory
