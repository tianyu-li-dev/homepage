## About Me

Greetings! I am a Ph.D. student in Supply Chain & Operations Management at Purdue University under the supervision of Professors [William B. Haskell](https://www.wbhaskell.com/) and [Chen-An Lin](https://web.ics.purdue.edu/~lin1800/index.html). I do operations management and [mathematical engineering](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matematik_m%C3%BChendisli%C4%9Fi). My research focuses on developing methods for solving sequential decision-making problems and applying them to real-world settings.

## Research Interests

- **Methodology:** dynamic programming and optimal control
- **Application:** stochastic modeling, inventory theory
